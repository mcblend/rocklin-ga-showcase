Parking

January 2026 rate, may differ
$8 per car, per day
  • Scan the QR code on the parking signs and pay in the HONK app. You'll need your plate number.
  • One payment covers the whole day. Pay again each day.
  • Unpaid cars got a windshield warning last time.
  • Carpool if you can. Lots fill up on busy days.

Weather & pack

Forecast as of Nov 8

Warm sun, cold nights

  • The sun is strong. There's little shade at the fields. Sunscreen before you arrive, then again between games.
  • It cools fast after sunset (about 5:25 PM). Saturday's late games and the drive back get cold. Bring a layer.
  • Drink early and often. Desert air dries you out. Players should hydrate the day before too.
  • Rain is unlikely. Small chance Friday. A light rain jacket covers it.
  • Mixed surfaces. Some fields are grass, some are turf.
